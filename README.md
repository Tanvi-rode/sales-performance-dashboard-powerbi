# Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales, profit, and order
performance across segments, categories, products, regions, and time.

![Dashboard](images)

📄 [View Dashboard PDF](report/Sales_Dashboard.pdf)

## Objective
Track overall sales performance, identify top-selling products and
customer segments, and understand sales trends over time to support
business decisions.

## Key KPIs
- Total Sales: $2.3M
- Total Profit: ~$286K
- Profit Margin: 12.5%
- Total Orders: 5K
- Total Quantity Sold: 38K
- Average Order Value: 458.61

## Visuals Included
- Total Sales by Segment (donut chart)
- Total Sales by Category (bar chart)
- Total Sales by Product Name (top products)
- Monthly Sales Trend (line chart)
- Slicers: Region, Category, Segment, and Year (2014 to 2017)

## Key Insights
- The Consumer segment brings in about half of all sales (50.5%),
  followed by Corporate (30.7%) and Home Office (18.7%).
- Technology is the top-selling category, with Furniture and
  Office Supplies close behind.
- A single product (Canon imageClass 2200) leads sales at roughly $60K,
  well ahead of the other top 10 products.
- Sales are lowest in February and rise sharply in the last quarter,
  peaking in November.
- A 12.5% profit margin shows healthy profitability overall.
